Dungeons and Dragons Alignments.

Lawful Good: (LG)
-"The Crusader." Combines honor with compassion.
Neutral Good (NG):
-"The Benefactor." Guided by conscience to help others without strict adherence to law.
Chaotic Good (CG):
- "The Rebel." Follows their own conscience, disregarding established rules.
Lawful Neutral (LN):
- "The Judge." Values reliability, order, and tradition above all.
True Neutral (N):
-"The Undecided." Avoids extremes; may prioritize self, nature, or balance.
Chaotic Neutral (CN):
-"The Free Spirit." Values freedom above all, following whims and disregarding rules.
Lawful Evil (LE):
-"The Dominator." Uses orderly systems to serve selfish, malicious ends.
Neutral Evil (NE):
-"The Malefactor." Pure selfishness; acts without regard for others.
Chaotic Evil (CE):
-"The Destroyer." Driven by greed, hatred, and impulsiveness, caring only for oneself.
