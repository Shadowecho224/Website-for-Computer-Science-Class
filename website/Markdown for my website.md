## 🐉 Dungeons and Dragons Content

### Alignments Quick Guide
* **Lawful Good (LG):** "The Crusader." Combines honor with compassion.
* **Neutral Good (NG):** "The Benefactor." Guided by conscience to help others.
* **Chaotic Good (CG):** "The Rebel." Follows their own conscience.
* **Lawful Neutral (LN):** "The Judge." Values reliability, order, and tradition.
* **True Neutral (N):** "The Undecided." Avoids extremes.
* **Chaotic Neutral (CN):** "The Free Spirit." Values freedom above all.
* **Lawful Evil (LE):** "The Dominator." Uses orderly systems for selfish ends.
* **Neutral Evil (NE):** "The Malefactor." Pure selfishness.
* **Chaotic Evil (CE):** "The Destroyer." Driven by greed, hatred, and impulsiveness.

### Featured Dragons
Our directory tracks multiple types of dragons including:
1. **Metallic Dragons** (Brass, Copper, Bronze, Gold, Silver)
2. **Gem Dragons** (Topaz, Emerald, Crystal, Sapphire, Amethyst)
3. **Chromatic Dragons** (Red, Black, Blue, Green, Brown, Grey, Deep, Purple, White)
